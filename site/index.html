Features

A library that listens to you.

Hand-crafted interactive stories, personalized nightly picks, and thoughtful tools that keep the story—not the interface—at the center.

🌿

Branching narratives

Stories unfold based on your decisions. Every reader walks a different path through the same world — and no two journeys end the same way.

✨

Create your own

Write a personal branching story by hand, or optionally generate one with your own securely stored Anthropic API key.

🏆

Endings tracker

Collect every ending. See which paths you've conquered and which secrets remain.

🎧

Living ambience

Soundscapes that shift with the scene — rain, fire, wind, wonder.

🗂️

Smart library

Favorites, curated mood collections, automatic progress, and search across titles, genres, synopses, and tags.

♿️

Accessible by design

VoiceOver support, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion, adjustable typography, focus mode, and legible light and dark palettes.

📱

iPhone and Android

Built natively for both worlds — SwiftUI on iOS, Jetpack Compose on Android. Fast, fluid, and at home on your device.
