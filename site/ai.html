How It Works

Five steps. A few seconds.

Understand the idea

Your prompt becomes a premise: who you are, what's at stake, and what kind of story this wants to be.

Architect the graph

The AI plans the skeleton first — scenes as nodes, choices as edges, endings as destinations worth reaching.

Write every scene

Each node gets prose with a voice that matches the genre, and choices with real consequences attached.

Validate every path

A story-graph validator walks every branch: no dead ends, no unreachable scenes, no choices that lead nowhere.

Hand you the book

The story lands in your library like any other — with ambience, endings tracking, and your name on the spine.
