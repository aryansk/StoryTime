Features

Built for readers
who can't sit still.

Every part of StoryTime is designed around one idea: a story should respond to you. Here's the full tour.

Interactive Reading

Stories that branch like real decisions.

Every scene ends in a choice, and every choice opens a different door. Paths split, cross back, and split again — some endings take three choices to reach, some take thirteen.

  • Branching narratives — hand-built story graphs, not linear chapters.
  • Multiple endings — triumphant, tragic, secret, and everything between.
  • Character relationships — who you charm or cross changes what's possible later.

Library

A library that organizes itself.

Save favorites, return to automatic checkpoints, and explore curator-built collections by mood, format, length, and genre.

  • Curated collections — quick, cozy, tense, epic, classic, and more.
  • Search that finds things — titles, genres, synopses, formats, and tags.
  • Reading progress — automatic checkpoints, percentages, stats, goals, and achievements.
Ambience

Soundscapes that follow the scene.

Optional genre ambience can sit beneath a scene while built-in narration reads it aloud. Both are easy to pause, tune, or leave completely off.

  • Genre ambience — subtle loops matched to each story's mood.
  • Built-in narration — adjustable speech rate with pause and resume.
  • One tap off — silence is always an option.

Endings Tracker

Collect every way it can end.

Each story shows you how many endings exist — and how many you've found. Finished paths light up; the rest stay teasingly dark until you earn them.

  • Per-story progress rings — see your completion at a glance.
  • Secret endings — some only appear after specific chains of choices.
  • Achievements — for the completionists who must find them all.
Reading Modes

Try the reading modes right here.

Tap a theme. The app also includes Dynamic Type, adjustable fonts and spacing, focus mode, and Reduce Motion support.

Accessibility

Great stories belong to everyone.

🗣️

VoiceOver, fully supported

Every scene, choice, and menu is labeled and navigable. The whole story works by ear.

🔠

Dynamic Type & custom fonts

Text scales to your system size, and you can swap typefaces for comfort or dyslexia-friendly reading.

🌗

High contrast modes

Ink and paper colors stay deliberately legible in both light and dark appearances.

🛡️

Age-appropriate filtering

Content controls let families decide which genres and intensity levels are on the shelf.
