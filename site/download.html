Before you turn the page.

The core library is free to read, with a growing shelf of free stories every month. A StoryTime+ subscription unlocks the full catalog and unlimited AI story generation.

Yes. Any story in your library is downloaded to your device — planes, trains, and dead zones are excellent places to make difficult choices.

Catalog stories are original "step inside" continuations and what-ifs inspired by films and series — you play a new character in that world, not a retelling of the plot. AI stories are 100% yours, generated from your idea.

StoryTime has age-appropriate content controls. Parents can filter by genre and intensity, and AI-generated stories respect the same limits.

Your reading progress, favorites, endings, and achievements are stored privately on the device you use. Cross-device sync is not currently available.

A single path usually takes 5–20 minutes. Longer stories reward replay, while Quick Reads fit into a short break.
